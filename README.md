# SURA
Summer Undergrad Research Award at IIT Delhi under Dr.Neel Kanth Kundu by Jay and Utkarsh

The primary objective of the Research is to improving the Indoor Localisation technology by exploiting IMU Sensors within smartphones for better and more physics based predictions.
The secondary objective is the use of Deep Learning Models (namely CNN and LSTM).

Also an experimental research for the use of quantum machine learning in the field of Indoor Localisation.

Dataset Used - I. Ashraf, S. Din, M. U. Ali, S. Hur, Y. B. Zikria and Y. Park, "MagWi: Benchmark Dataset for Long Term Magnetic Field and Wi-Fi Data Involving Heterogeneous Smartphones, Multiple Orientations, Spatial Diversity and Multi-Floor Buildings," in IEEE Access, vol. 9, pp. 77976-77996, 2021, doi: 10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3083662.
